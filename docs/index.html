SubWatch

Track all your subscriptions in one place. Never waste money on forgotten recurring payments.

Install Free — Chrome Web Store

Features

  • See total monthly & yearly subscription spending at a glance
  • Get renewal reminders before you're charged
  • Track free trial deadlines — never get surprise charges
  • Color-coded status: urgent (red), soon (yellow), safe (green)
  • Export your subscription list to CSV
  • Syncs across all your Chrome devices
  • 100% private — no bank connection, no tracking, no analytics

Why SubWatch?

The average person has 8+ active subscriptions costing $219/month — but thinks they only spend $86. Americans waste $27.7 billion per year on forgotten subscriptions.

SubWatch shows you the real number. No bank login required. No subscription to track your subscriptions.
