SubWatch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

What data does SubWatch collect?

SubWatch stores your subscription data (names, costs, renewal dates, categories) locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage API. This data syncs across your devices via your Chrome account if you have Chrome Sync enabled.

What data does SubWatch NOT collect?

  • No personal information (name, email, address)
  • No financial data (bank accounts, credit cards)
  • No browsing history
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No third-party services or APIs

Data sharing

SubWatch does not share, sell, or transmit any data to any third party. All data stays in your browser.

Permissions

  • storage: Save your subscription list locally and sync across Chrome devices
  • alarms: Schedule renewal reminder checks
  • notifications: Send renewal reminders before charges

Data deletion

Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data. You can also delete individual subscriptions or all data from within the extension.
