## Long Description (SEO keyword-rich)

SubWatch is a subscription tracker and subscription manager that helps you track all your subscriptions in one place. Get renewal reminders before you're charged, see your monthly and yearly subscription spending, and cancel free trials before they convert to paid plans. Stop wasting money on forgotten recurring payments.

FEATURES:

★ Subscription Dashboard — See all your subscriptions at a glance with total monthly and yearly cost. Your personal subscription spending tracker with color-coded renewal status.

★ Renewal Reminders — Get notifications 1, 3, or 7 days before any subscription renews. Never forget to cancel a free trial again. Built-in recurring payment reminder that works automatically.

★ Smart Categories — Organize subscriptions by type: Streaming (Netflix, Disney+), Music (Spotify, Apple Music), Software & SaaS (ChatGPT, Figma, Adobe), Cloud Storage (iCloud, Google One), Gaming (Xbox, PlayStation), and more.

★ Free Trial Tracker — Mark subscriptions as free trials and get special alerts before they convert to paid. The easiest way to track free trial deadlines and avoid unwanted charges.

★ Spending Calculator — See exactly how much you spend on subscriptions per month and per year. Most people underestimate their subscription costs by 2.5x. SubWatch shows the real number.

★ Export Subscriptions — Download your subscription list as CSV for budgeting. Works with Excel, Google Sheets, and any spreadsheet app.

★ Badge Counter — See your total monthly subscription cost right on the extension icon. A quick glance tells you how much you're spending on recurring charges.

★ Sync Across Devices — All your subscription data syncs via Chrome account. Add a subscription on your laptop, see it on your desktop.

★ 100% Private — All data stored locally and in your Chrome sync. No bank connection needed. No third-party access. Unlike Rocket Money or Truebill, SubWatch never asks for your financial login.

WHO IS SUBWATCH FOR?

• Anyone who uses streaming services like Netflix, Spotify, YouTube Premium, Disney+, HBO Max
• Developers and designers tracking SaaS subscriptions (GitHub Copilot, Figma, Adobe CC, Notion, ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro)
• Freelancers managing multiple recurring business expenses
• Students tracking education subscriptions and free trial deadlines
• Anyone who wants a simple subscription budget tracker without linking bank accounts

The average person has 8+ active subscriptions costing $219/month but thinks they only spend $86. Americans waste $27.7 billion per year on forgotten subscriptions. SubWatch helps you see the real picture and take control of your recurring payments.

FREE to use. No account required. No bank connection needed. Just install, add your subscriptions, and start saving money.
